  paragraphs:
    - "I am Lara Bordalo, an author and picture book maker based in Portugal. I believe we need both sun and rain to live fully, so I try to put both on the page."
    - "I work in mixed media, combining paper cut, painting and digital techniques to find fresh shapes with a warm, handmade feeling. I like to blend textures, linework, warm palettes and playful forms. My inspiration springs from childhood memories and the rich diversity of my roots in Brazil, Portugal and Spain."
    - "Words and images have been with me since I was nine, when a text and illustration of mine was one of thirty selected for a book collection by Editora Melhoramentos. In fifth grade I was a finalist in the Portuguese Language Olympics “Escrevendo o Futuro” in São Paulo."
    - "Before illustration I worked in music, as a co-producer and on projects such as Rock in Rio and Red Bull Favela Beats. Today I focus entirely on illustration, especially books for children. Whatever the medium, my role has always been the same: to tell stories."
